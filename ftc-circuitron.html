Emergency details and fire extinguisher guidance

In an emergency

Stop work, alert the supervising mentor and keep others clear. For fire or smoke, raise the alarm and evacuate by the nearest safe exit. Go to the designated assembly point and stay out until cleared. For serious injury, fire or immediate danger in India, call 112 and give the exact site address. Get the trained first aider and follow emergency-dispatch instructions. At competitions, alert event staff and follow venue procedures.

Extinguisher use is for trained adults only, with the correct extinguisher, a small contained fire and a clear escape route. Students evacuate.

Personal protection and workshop conduct
  • Wear rated safety glasses with side protection during shop and robot work. Use suitable splash goggles when required, including over ordinary prescription glasses. Use a face shield over primary eye protection where the task requires it.
  • Wear closed-toe, closed-heel shoes. Tie back long hair and secure loose clothing. Remove dangling jewellery.
  • Never wear gloves near exposed rotating machinery, including drill presses and lathes. Use task-appropriate gloves for handling sharp stationary stock or chemicals only where the equipment procedure and safety data sheet allow it. Use hearing protection and dust controls as required.
  • Keep exits, aisles and floors clear. Anyone can call “Stop” if conditions are unsafe. Report injuries, near misses and faulty tools to the supervising mentor before restarting.
Power tools and machines
  1. Use only equipment you have been trained and authorised to operate. Students work under mentor supervision. Follow the machine-specific instructions and use approved stock and tooling.
  2. Locate the stop control. Check guards, cords, bits and blades. Secure the workpiece with a vise or clamps and clear the area. Stop and report damaged equipment.
  3. Keep hands away from moving parts. Stop the tool and wait for motion to cease. Follow the machine’s energy-isolation procedure before changing tooling, clearing a jam, or performing maintenance. Clear chips with a brush after motion stops.
Robot testing, operation and repair
  • Use a designated test area with a clear boundary. Assign a Driver Station operator and spotter. Check battery retention, loose parts and the planned motion. Announce before enabling and wait until everyone is clear.
  • If the robot becomes unsafe, the operator uses the Driver Station STOP control. If it does not respond, keep everyone clear and use the main power switch only when safe to reach. At an event, alert field staff. Keep people outside the robot’s range until it is safe.
  • Before working on the robot, stop the OpMode, turn off the main power switch and unplug the battery. Safely support raised mechanisms and secure or release stored energy in springs and other moving parts. A software stop alone does not isolate energy.
  • Use a cart and an agreed team lift when needed. Charge batteries at the designated station using approved chargers. Stop using damaged or leaking batteries and notify the mentor. Keep metal tools clear of terminals.
Practice and training checks

Before each session, check PPE, tools, exits, batteries and the test area. Give students practical tool training and record competence. Log faults and near misses and resolve them before restarting affected work.
